# Player website glossary

Language for discussing this goalkeeper's website and its audiences.

## Language

**Player website**:
The public website presenting the player's football journey, playing information, and materials to football professionals and followers.
_Avoid_: Marketing platform, fan platform

**Player**:
The project owner's cousin, a goalkeeper in Colombia, whose career and personality the player website presents.

**Football professional**:
A visitor considering the player in a football capacity, such as a coach or scout. Their needs take priority when they conflict with those of other audiences.

**Follower**:
A visitor interested in following the player's story and progress, without necessarily evaluating him for a football opportunity.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Football opportunity**:
An enquiry or prospect concerning the player's football development or playing career. An enquiry does not establish a confirmed recruitment, affiliation, or signing.
