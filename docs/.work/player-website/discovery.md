# Player website discovery

Source notes from the initial wayfinder conversation. These are planning context, not approved website copy or a finished specification. Continue the exploration through [the map](map.md); subsequent ticket answers hold subsequent decisions.

## Agreed destination

Agree on a direction for the website's first version: purpose, audience, content, visual approach, and maintenance needs. Identify missing family information so a build specification can follow.

## Context reported by the project owner

- The website is for the owner's cousin, a goalkeeper in Colombia.
- The owner reports that he has traveled and played in different places around Colombia and is attracting attention from coaches, scouts, and a growing audience.
- The owner has heard that a Colombia national team player watched him. The identity, circumstances, and permission to mention this are unknown.
- The owner has heard of a possible recruitment process involving "Cali." The club, stage, and accuracy are unknown. Do not interpret this as a confirmed club affiliation or signing.
- The owner wants to support his cousin's exposure and professional presentation, and also develop a project for his own portfolio.
- The family has limited financial means and is supporting the player's football ambitions. This is private planning context, not approved public storytelling.
- The player's mother has a rough awareness of the website idea. The player's wishes and the family's approval of specific public content have not yet been gathered.

## Working preferences

- Explain sports and marketing choices in plain language. The owner does not have prior expertise in these areas.
- Work with available information, distinguish reports from confirmed facts, and do not ask the owner to guess missing details.
- When a decision requires the player's or parents' knowledge, use the questionnaire workflow to gather it.
- No family questionnaire has been drafted or sent yet.

## Initial direction agreed with the project owner

- Serve football professionals and followers with roughly equal care, with football opportunities taking priority when their needs conflict. Seek family feedback on this priority.
- Explore the player's football development, personality, and ambitions. Invite the family to choose the amount of personal context they want public.
- Support Spanish and English, with Spanish as the more important language.
- The owner will maintain the website initially. Keep recurring costs minimal.
- A family content dashboard and handover to a marketing person or team are possible future directions, not first-version commitments.
- Explore a professional but fun player profile with strong action photography and personality. Seek inspiration from official athlete sites, particularly Max Verstappen and Lando Norris, and get family feedback on the visual direction.

## Family information to collect later

Known gaps include the player's preferred public name, age, current team and career status, wishes for the website, available photos and footage, permission to use materials, accurate player information, and the appropriate contact for opportunities. Also seek family feedback on the audience priority, personal storytelling, and visual direction. The questionnaire should distinguish answers they know, facts needing confirmation, material they can share now, and material they may gather later.
