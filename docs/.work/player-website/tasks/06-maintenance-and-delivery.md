Blocked by: 04

## Question

What delivery and content-maintenance approach fits the first version, minimal recurring costs, owner-managed updates, and a possible future handover?

Choose how bilingual text, footage, photos, and updates will be maintained; how opportunity contacts will be handled; and what hosting or external services the content actually requires. Verify current product capabilities and costs before making provider-specific recommendations. Prefer a proportionate approach, and explain trade-offs without assuming technical expertise. Keep a family dashboard out of this first version. Record a practical handover path; use an ADR only when the domain-modeling criteria justify one.
