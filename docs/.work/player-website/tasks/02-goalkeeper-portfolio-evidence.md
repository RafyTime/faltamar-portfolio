## Answer

Resolved on 5 October 2026. [Useful evidence for a goalkeeper portfolio](../research/goalkeeper-portfolio-evidence.md) records primary-source examples from FIFA, clubs, and a federation talent pathway, with their applicability limits.

The source-informed proposal is accurate player information, varied goalkeeper footage, longer match context when available, and an agreed opportunity contact. FIFA analysis gives useful examples beyond saves: positioning, crosses, one-on-ones, support to defenders, and distribution. This is coaching context, not a universal scout checklist. No universal reel duration, height threshold, mandatory statistic, or proven recruitment benefit from a website was established.

The family questionnaire should request existing materials and confirmed facts first, identifying sources, dates, public-content preferences, and the contact person. It should not demand costly filming or invented statistics. The source examples do not establish this player's club affiliation or recruitment status.
