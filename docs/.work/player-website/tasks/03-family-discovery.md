# Gather the player's and parents' input

Status: needs-info
Type: task
Labels: wayfinder:task
Parent: [Find the direction for the player website](../map.md)
Blocked by: none

## Question

Gather the family's preferences, confirmed player information, and an initial inventory of materials so the website's content and presentation can be chosen with their input.

Use the to-questionnaire workflow. Recipients are the player and his parents, the owner's relatives in Colombia. They know his career circumstances, wishes, and available materials. Draft in approachable Spanish unless the owner requests otherwise. Ask about his preferred public name, age, current team and career status, football profile, ambitions, existing social accounts, contact for opportunities, available photos and footage, sources and permission to use them, and information they want kept private. Request feedback on audience priority, personal storytelling, and the proposed professional but fun direction. Include an easy way to say "I don't know" or "we need to check."

Do not require sensitive documents or a complete asset library to answer. Ask for existing links and a small initial sample; record missing material separately. The owner shares the questionnaire, not an agent. Resolve when returned answers provide enough confirmed input to proceed, recording residual gaps explicitly.
