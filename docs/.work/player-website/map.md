# Find the direction for the player website

Labels: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Agree on the first version's purpose, audience, content, visual approach, and maintenance needs. Identify missing family information so a build specification can follow.

## Notes

- Tracker: local Markdown, following `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`. Child tickets are in this folder's `tasks/` directory; blocking uses their `Blocked by:` fields.
- Consult wayfinder when resuming; grilling and domain-modeling for conversations; research for source investigations; prototype for visual exploration; to-questionnaire when gathering family input. Apply unslop to writing.
- [Initial discovery](discovery.md) records the agreed starting direction, user context, and unconfirmed reports. It is not public website copy.
- Explain football, marketing, and technical trade-offs in plain language. Do not expect the owner to supply expertise they do not have.
- The player and parents provide their own preferences and factual answers. The owner cannot approve those on their behalf.
- Drafting questionnaires is in scope; sending messages is not authorized. The owner shares them and brings back answers.
- Use `claimed` and `resolved` for active and completed wayfinder tickets as required by the tracker. Other ticket statuses use the triage labels.
- This map plans the website. It does not implement or publish it. Resolve at most one non-research ticket per working session.

## Decisions so far

- [Learn from official athlete websites](tasks/01-athlete-site-inspiration.md): verified references suggest distinct football and personal paths, strong photography, and manageable playfulness for prototype discussion.
- [Identify useful goalkeeper portfolio evidence](tasks/02-goalkeeper-portfolio-evidence.md): source-informed content and footage examples are ready to inform the questionnaire, with no universal scout checklist assumed.

## Not yet specified

- The player's own preferences and confirmed career facts may change which content deserves emphasis.
- The available footage and photographs will shape the design and the amount of content feasible at launch.
- Family answers may reveal additional questions about public information, opportunity handling, or materials.
- Research and a visual prototype may reveal interactions worth considering for a professional but fun presentation.
- Final scope and acceptance criteria will become clear after the content, visual, and maintenance decisions.

## Out of scope

- Building, deploying, or publishing the website during this exploration.
- Building a family content dashboard in the first version. Revisit it when actual update needs justify it.
- Delivering a marketing team's operating setup now. Explore affordable maintenance and a practical future handover instead.
- Fundraising, sponsorship sales, merchandise, or representation services. These have not been requested.
- Public claims about a signing, club affiliation, or endorsement based on the owner's unconfirmed reports.
